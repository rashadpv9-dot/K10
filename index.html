About Kingston 10

Caribbean soul. Bahraini heart.

We are Bahrain's first authentic Caribbean restaurant, bringing the spicy, smoky flavours of Jamaica to Block 338, Adliya.

Our name comes from Kingston, Jamaica's vibrant capital. Our founder grew up surrounded by jerk chicken, reggae and open tables — and when we came to Bahrain, we missed that unapologetic Caribbean flavour. So we brought it ourselves.

  • From scratchTraditional recipes passed down through generations. No shortcuts, no compromises.
  • Island spiceSpices imported straight from the islands, cooked with love and fire.
  • Like familyGreat food, great music, great energy — and always a warm welcome.
